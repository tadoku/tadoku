Quieter fields, responsive actions, abstract marks.

This pass keeps the 2px interaction cue but lowers its contrast on pale fields. The primary action retains its authority and now has a visible hover state. The logo study starts over without letters.

01 · Control refinement

The same thickness, less visual weight

Inputs keep a two-pixel lower edge for tactility, but it now uses a mid-neutral rather than the near-black rule. Submit buttons retain their darker violet lower edge because the filled surface can support the contrast.

02 · Abstract logo reset

No initials. No letter puzzles.

These are five fresh territories, not revisions of the previous marks. They avoid forcing a T or K and instead explore turning a page, opening a space, accumulating reading, forward motion, and the rhythm of a marked page.

1 · Page Turn

A single sheet caught mid-turn. The violet fold is the moment of movement, while the outer shape stays archival and square.

Character: quiet, editorial, precise.

2 · Open Passage

Two planes create a narrow violet passage between them. It suggests opening a book or entering another world without drawing either literally.

Character: spatial, immersive, book-adjacent.

3 · Accumulation

Three reading units gather into a stack. The stepped ends communicate steady progress rather than completion or competition.

Character: patient, cumulative, system-friendly.

4 · Reading Current

A line moves forward, doubles back, and continues. It represents the relaxed Tadoku loop: encounter, absorb, keep going.

Character: active, continuous, less obviously literary.

5 · Marginal Rhythm

A marked margin beside lines of changing length. It references reading traces and the interface accent rail without becoming a document icon.

Character: archival, rhythmic, closest to the UI language.
No hidden initials
