Bookplate refinement · third study

Same library. After dark.

Dark mode keeps the paper-and-ink hierarchy rather than simply inverting it. Surfaces become layered charcoal, rules stay neutral, and ink violet maps to a lighter tonal partner so the brand remains visible and accessible.

Ink violet retained#5747B8 in light mode maps to #9A8BEA in dark mode: same identity, appropriate luminance.
Accent owns its edgeA colored left marker replaces the neutral hairline instead of sitting inside it.
Softer interactionControls stay 1px on every side; only the bottom border color becomes darker.
Logo rendering fixedThe original even-odd fill and independent accent wedge are restored.
01 / Dark mode

Semantic tokens change tone, not structure.

Dark mode uses charcoal surfaces rather than black, avoiding both glare and the “developer console” look. Structural relationships remain identical in both modes.

02 / Accent edge

The accent replaces the left border.

There is no neutral hairline underneath or outside the marker. The other three sides retain the standard component rule.

Recommended

The violet edge is the boundary. Top, right, and bottom remain neutral.

Previous preview

A violet stripe sits inside a fully outlined component, creating a doubled left edge and awkward corners.

Rule: a component may have a neutral four-sided border or an accented left edge with three neutral sides—never both left edges.
