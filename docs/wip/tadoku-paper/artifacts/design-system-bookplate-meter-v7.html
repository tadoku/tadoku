Approved direction

Include hard-edged elevation in the design language. Use 3px floating depth for overlays and 5px showcase depth sparingly. Ordinary cards stay flat so elevation continues to communicate layer and importance.

02 · Compact mark

A meter family, tested without color

The Bookmeter reference succeeds because three growing bars communicate accumulation immediately. These are original Tadoku-oriented variations on that broad idea—not redraws of its exact proportions. Every hero mark below is monochrome.

What to borrow as a principle: very few shapes, a clear rhythm, and meaning visible in silhouette. What not to borrow: Bookmeter’s exact three-bar proportions or its green treatment. Tadoku needs its own construction.

Non-negotiable logo rule

Black first. Reverse second. Accent third.

The selected mark must be distinctive as a single-color SVG, work reversed on a dark field, and remain legible at 16px. Ink violet may emphasize one existing piece of the construction, but it cannot create the construction or rescue its meaning.
