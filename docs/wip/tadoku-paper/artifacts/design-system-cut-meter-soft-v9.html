Tadoku compact mark · Study 9

What if the cut were actually soft?

The previous round rounded the ends of one long line, but those ends lived outside the bars. This round places rounded geometry inside every bar, where it materially changes the silhouette.

Construction correction

Each visible piece of the cut is now its own capsule, arc, or circle. That preserves the upward rhythm while making the softness visible in monochrome and at small sizes.
