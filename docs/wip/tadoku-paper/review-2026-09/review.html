Three decisions to review together

1. Brand minimum size and clear space

The packaged wordmark and Cut Meter are restored and demonstrated, but there is no accepted numerical minimum size or clear-space rule. Example dimensions are not a new brand standard.

Options: approve universal minimums now, or inspect the intended header, mobile and compact placements before choosing them. Recommendation: review those real placements first, then record a consistent rule in Brand.

2. CJK typography coverage

Open Sans and Merriweather cover the shipped Latin typography. Japanese and other scripts use available system fallbacks, so glyph metrics differ between platforms.

Options: retain platform fallbacks, or choose and bundle language fonts for consistent rendering with a larger font payload. Recommendation: retain the documented fallback for now and compare Japanese reading titles on the target devices before adding fonts.

3. Logging v2: review, saving and contest submission

The experimental example preserves work/pages when moving between editing and review. It deliberately does not claim persistence or contest submission.

Decisions: is review mandatory, are saving reading and submitting to a contest separate actions, and what determines eligibility? Language, reading date, units and real pending/error/success states need that chosen flow.

Recommendation: keep the page Experimental and agree the complete reading-entry flow before treating the prototype as a production specification.

Two existing choices retained

Segmented controls: keep wrapping on narrow screens, as the current component supports it; use short labels and a simpler choice control when a wrapped set becomes hard to scan. Flash rails: retain the existing 4px status rail, distinct from the 3px foundation accent rail. These are recorded for review, but neither requires changing the component contract in this pass.
